# Demo: Quartz Before and After

This is a small product demo showing the same content slice in two different site experiences:

- [Before: Current Site Slice](quartz-before-current-slice.md)
- [After: Quartz-Style Slice](quartz-after-style-slice.md)

The point is not pixel-perfect imitation.
The point is to make the product difference concrete.

## What This Demo Is Showing

- The **before** page shows the current HB Robotics style: problem-first, curated, concise, with explicit onward links.
- The **after** page shows a **Quartz-style** presentation: note metadata, backlinks, local graph, and more note-to-note exploration behavior.

## What To Compare

- Does the Quartz-style version make exploration feel better or just busier?
- Are backlinks and graph-style navigation actually useful for this repo's readers?
- Does the current version do a better job of getting a robot builder to the next useful answer with fewer distractions?
- Would Quartz improve the experience enough to justify a site migration and workflow change?

## Recommended Comparison Flow

1. Open the [Before page](quartz-before-current-slice.md).
2. Open the [After page](quartz-after-style-slice.md).
3. Ask which one better fits a person arriving with a concrete robotics problem versus a person exploring a connected note system.
