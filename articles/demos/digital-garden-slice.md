## Backlinks-Style Context

This page would feel more like a note in a web of notes.
Instead of asking the reader to stay inside one official section, it would encourage movement across Navigation, Foundations, Hardware, and Perception as soon as the problem requires it.

Likely product traits:

- lighter sense of chapter order
- heavier use of related links and backlinks
- more note-like landing pages
- more acceptance that readers arrive in the middle

## Reader Experience

Good fit if you want:

- a knowledge-base feel
- exploratory browsing
- more serendipitous discovery
- weaker boundaries between sections

Risk:

- new readers may feel less guided
- section ownership becomes fuzzier
- public URLs and hierarchy matter a bit less than note connectivity
