# Site Direction Demos

This section is a small product demo rather than a permanent article section.
It shows the same robotics knowledge slice rendered in two different site styles, plus a separate comparison of current URLs versus a cleaner public structure.

## What To Compare

- [Digital-Garden Slice](digital-garden-slice.md): looser exploration, backlink-style movement, and note-cluster discovery.
- [Traditional Docs Slice](traditional-docs-slice.md): stronger hierarchy, curated entry points, and a clearer learning path.
- [URL and Section Structure Demo](url-and-section-structure-demo.md): current public shape versus a more deliberately curated section and slug scheme.

## How To Use These Demos

- Open the digital-garden and traditional-docs pages side by side.
- Ask which one better matches how you want a reader to arrive, orient, and continue.
- Then use the URL demo page to decide whether you want to keep the current article paths or invest in a cleaner public information architecture.
